#### How I work

I keep the bar small and checkable:

- **Name the outcome.** What should be true when this is finished? Vague "done" is not a finish line.
- **Agree the signals.** What would we look at to know it worked — a measure, a gate, a staging check — before we celebrate.
- **Own the path.** One clear owner for the tip; handoffs are explicit, not assumed.
- **Prefer quality over theater.** Staging and gates beat a rushed ship that nobody can trust.
- **Learn without blame.** When something breaks, we write what happened and what changes next — specific and actionable, not a hunt for a villain.

Feedback I find useful is goal-oriented, specific, and actionable. That is the voice I try to use on my own work too.
