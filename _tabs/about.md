I develop a website that provides software as a service.

People open it in a browser, sign in, and use software I keep running. There is a place to arrive, a way to get in, and a service they return to. This site is the record of that work: the shape of the website, the account that makes it theirs, and the fact that it stays available.

The theme is [Chirpy](https://github.com/cotes2020/jekyll-theme-chirpy), used here as the reading layout.
